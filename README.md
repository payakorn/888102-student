# 888102 Big Data Analytics -- Chapter 10 Materials

International College of Digital Innovation, Chiang Mai University.

This repo holds the Chapter 10 notebooks and the interactive in-browser
practice decks for course 888102. It does not include lecture slides, the
exam bank, other chapters, or any instructor material -- ask your instructor
for those separately.

## Notebooks

Each `ch10_N_*.ipynb` is one part of Chapter 10: theory, worked examples,
and a graded homework section. Open one directly in Colab:

- Click a notebook's Colab badge below, or
- Go to https://colab.research.google.com/github/payakorn/888102-student, sign in, and pick
  the notebook from the file browser.
